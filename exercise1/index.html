Cristiano Ronaldo

Born February 5, 1985 — still breaking records today

About Cristiano Ronaldo

Cristiano Ronaldo dos Santos Aveiro was born on February 5, 1985, in Funchal, Madeira, Portugal. He rose from a working-class background to become one of the most decorated footballers in history, playing for clubs like Sporting CP, Manchester United, Real Madrid, Juventus, and Al Nassr. Known for his relentless work ethic, athleticism, and goal-scoring ability, he has won multiple Ballon d'Or awards and league titles across different countries. Off the pitch, he is also recognized as one of the most followed athletes in the world on social media.

Key Career Events

  1. 2002 — Joined Sporting CP's youth academy
  2. 2003 — Signed with Manchester United
  3. 2009 — Transferred to Real Madrid for a then world-record fee
  4. 2016 — Won the UEFA European Championship with Portugal
  5. 2018 — Moved to Juventus
  6. 2023 — Signed with Al Nassr in Saudi Arabia

Key Achievements

  • Multiple Ballon d'Or awards
  • Champions League titles with Manchester United and Real Madrid
  • All-time top scorer in international football
  • UEFA European Championship winner with Portugal (2016)
  • Countless domestic league titles and top-scorer awards
